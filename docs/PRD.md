# DormDrop - Product Requirements Document

## Project Overview

DormDrop is a campus marketplace where college students can buy, sell, donate, and exchange items within their campus community.

The goal is to provide a clean, modern, and trustworthy marketplace exclusively for students.

---

# Target Users

- College Students
- Freshers
- Seniors
- Hostel Students

---

# Problem Statement

Students frequently need to sell or purchase items like:

- Books
- Electronics
- Furniture
- Lab Equipment
- Hostel Essentials

Existing marketplaces are cluttered and lack campus-specific trust.

DormDrop solves this problem.
